The pack itself says the images are AI-generated concept art, not a
production atlas. Origin and rights were not independently verified; they
carry no author, name, logo or text from Boom Online. Confirm ownership and
have art QA normalise the frames before a commercial release.

tools/slice_robot.gd cut the 16 frames into assets/boss/clockwork_bot/
walk_<dir>/01..04.png (256x256, one scale for all, feet on the bottom edge)
and idle/01.png (= walk_down/01). Specks of the sheet's halo (blobs under 3 %
of the robot) were removed. No pixels were painted. Robot bombs in game use
the project's own assets/bombs/strong_black.png.
