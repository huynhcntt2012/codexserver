tools/slice_ui_kit.gd cut them into apps/practice/assets/ui/kit: whole
stickers (icon_*.png), blank pill buttons rebuilt from each button's own left
cap and clean middle column (button_*.png; ghost, lavender and off are the
blue pill recoloured) and the small icon at the start of each button keyed off
its body colour (glyph_*.png). The words baked into the kit buttons are not
used; labels are drawn by Godot. Only cutting, keying, recolouring and
scaling; no pixels painted.
