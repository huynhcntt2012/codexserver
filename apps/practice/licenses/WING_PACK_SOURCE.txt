# Modular Wing Pack (wings and halos)

Source: one sheet image, "MODULAR WING PACK", supplied by the project owner in
a Claude Code session on 2026-10-18, kept at `tools/source/modular_wing_pack.webp`
(675x843 RGBA WebP).
SHA-256: 8f9b69d5a13f30d18b126ce997bd68ef409612504deb191f52a78865f1e4b930

The sheet looks like image-generator output made for this project's five heroes
(the same owner sent a matching hero sheet with "wing attach point" notes). Its
origin and rights were not independently verified; it carries no author, name,
logo or text from Boom Online. Confirm ownership before a commercial release.

Six sets: blue_angel, pink_heart, red_flame, golden_light, green_nature,
white_classic. `tools/slice_wing_pack.gd` cut each set into:
- `assets/cosmetics/halos/<set>.png`: the halo panel.
- `assets/cosmetics/wings/<set>/idle/01-04.png` and `flap/01-04.png`.
Only the alpha was remapped (the sheet's panels are ~0% alpha) and slivers of
neighbouring frames were cleared; no pixels were painted. The "Front/Side/Back"
and "Attach Point" panels were not used.
