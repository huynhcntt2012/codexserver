# Rider sprites (heroes sitting on a pet)

Source: one sheet image "RIDER CHARACTER SHEET" (1122x1402, opaque WebP),
supplied by the project owner in a Claude Code session on 2026-10-19, kept at
`tools/source/rider_sheet.webp` (413828 bytes).
SHA-256: c39a88bfa44594151daed6cc836e229c80fc612c2bff2494a9bc370a26a74599

Five columns match the project's heroes (blue boy = bombo, pink girl = mimi,
red boy = zin, golden girl = lala, green boy = kai); five rows of 3 frames:
mounted idle, ride down, ride up, ride left, ride right. It looks like
image-generator output made for this project's heroes; its origin and rights
were not independently verified, and it carries no author, name, logo or text
from Boom Online. Confirm ownership before a commercial release.

`tools/slice_rider_sheet.gd` removed each row's coloured panel (region growing
from the panel edge), split the 3 frames at the emptiest columns, dropped
slivers of neighbouring frames and scaled every hero's frames by one factor
into `assets/riders/<hero>/<anim>/01-03.png` (64x64, seat on the bottom edge).
No pixels were painted. The attach-point and usage panels were not used.
