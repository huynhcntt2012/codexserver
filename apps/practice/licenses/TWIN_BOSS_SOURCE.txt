# Cactus King and Thunder Ram boss art

Source: "Boom_Boss_Cuu_Sam_Set_Vua_Xuong_Rong_Dev_Spec.pdf" (19 pages, spec
v1.0 "Boss Đấu Trường Cừu Sấm Sét & Vua Xương Rồng"), supplied by the project
owner in a Claude Code session on 2026-10-20.
PDF SHA-256: 1efb3396b1dda9dcad9db146bd70f249495e511a947718a0d207565fada97a36

Its embedded images (RGB + alpha mask) were extracted with poppler pdfimages,
merged with their masks and kept as WebP in apps/practice/tools/source:
- thunder_ram_move.webp (page 10: down, left, right, up rows)
- thunder_ram_skills.webp (page 11: charge, thunder ball, cross, storm)

The spec says the images are AI-generated concepts; origin and rights were not
independently verified, and they carry no author, name, logo or text from Boom
Online. Confirm ownership before a commercial release.

tools/slice_twin_bosses.gd cut the Thunder Ram bodies into
assets/boss/thunder_ram/walk_<dir>/NN.png and idle/NN.png (one
scale per boss, 256 px canvas) and chosen skill frames into vfx/<name>.png.
Only cutting, removing slivers of neighbouring frames and scaling; no pixels
were painted.
