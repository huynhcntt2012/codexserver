Confirm ownership before a commercial release.

tools/slice_slimes.gd cut three rows into assets/boss/<set>/{move,idle,cast,
defeat}/01.png: slime_king ("Crowned Elite", 320x320, the boss), mini_bomb
("Mini Slime Bomb", 160x160, thrown at hit warnings) and sticky ("Sticky
Slime", 160x160, thrown at Block Lane). Slivers of neighbouring frames were
removed and each set scaled by one factor. No pixels were painted. The other
five minions are not used yet.
