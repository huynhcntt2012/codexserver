# Pet sheets (pet skins)

Source: `asset.7z` supplied by the project owner in a Claude Code session on
2026-10-19 (SHA-256 959afb9f50293d9b5299ea4af470c00c2ba4926345bf06d915866d92dbeebad7),
holding ten sprite sheets 229.png-238.png (1122x1402 RGBA PNG, ~2 MB each). The
sheets are not committed; keep the archive to re-cut them.

Each sheet is 4 columns x 5 rows: idle, walk_down, walk_up, walk_left,
walk_right. They look like image-generator output (cute animals with saddles);
their origin and rights were not independently verified, and they carry no
author, name, logo or text from Boom Online. Confirm ownership before a
commercial release.

Sheet -> pet: 229 bunny, 230 dragon, 231 sheep, 232 deer, 233 alpaca, 234 cat,
235 corgi, 236 dino, 237 turtle, 238 fox.

`tools/slice_pet_sheets.gd` cut every cell into
`assets/cosmetics/pets/<pet>/<anim>/01-04.png` (128x128). Only the alpha was
remapped (faint glow and specks removed), small blobs and slivers of
neighbouring cells were cleared, edge colour was bled into transparent pixels
before scaling; no pixels were painted. Each frame was moved sideways so the
art's alpha-weighted centre is the frame centre (the art drifted across each
row of the sheet, which made the pet slide left and right while animating).
